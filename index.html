About Nord Lane

Clothes that earn their place in the closet.

Nord Lane began as a two-person studio sewing overshirts for friends who cycled to work in all weather. The idea has not changed much: fewer pieces, better cloth, and patterns tested through a full winter before anyone else sees them.

We buy wool from small mills, use recycled shell fabrics where they perform, and cut our trousers and coats with extra seam allowance so they can be altered and repaired for years.

Wear-tested

Every sample is worn for eight weeks of real commutes before we approve the pattern.

Repairable

Send back any Nord Lane garment and we re-stitch, re-hem or patch it for a flat $10.

Fewer, longer

Twelve core pieces per season. Colors return each year so nothing goes out of date.
